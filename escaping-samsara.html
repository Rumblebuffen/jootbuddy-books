Escaping Samsara

Conversations with Three Generations of Ashtanga Teachers

Nathan Thompson

Foreword by Harmony Slater · Afterword by Russell Altice Case

Thirty-one long-form conversations with Ashtanga teachers spanning three generations of the practice — from those who studied directly in Mysore under K. Pattabhi Jois to the teachers now carrying that lineage forward, adapting it, and in some cases breaking from it entirely. The book follows the threads that connect them: lineage and authority, the body as it ages, doubt, devotion, and what actually happens on the mat after the romance of it wears off.

About the author

Nathan Thompson

Nathan Thompson is a journalist and the host of the Escaping Samsara podcast and Substack. He has practised Ashtanga yoga for around fifteen years, working through the advanced series, which is what sent him looking for teachers willing to talk honestly about what the practice does — and doesn’t do — over decades. Escaping Samsara is his first book.
